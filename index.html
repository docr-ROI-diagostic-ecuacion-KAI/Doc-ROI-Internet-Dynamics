SESSION 1

The core idea: Internet automated the communication process.

Learning objective

Many organizations see Internet as a set of channels, campaigns or information systems. Internet Dynamics proposes a different view: Internet can be studied as a living communication system where users, content and infrastructure generate feedback, acceleration, delay and measurable behavior over time.

Methodological rule

First identify the communication system. Then measure its endogenous variables, observe the external frictions, and only then simulate its evolution.

Key concepts: communication system · feedback loop · users · content · infrastructure · Forrester · AI arrival model

Forrester, J. W. (1961). Industrial Dynamics. MIT Press. Shannon, C. E. and Weaver, W. (1949). The Mathematical Theory of Communication.

2 · System dynamics

Forrester gives us the language: stocks, flows, feedback, delays and reinforcing loops.

Internet Dynamics applies system dynamics to a digital communication system. If users grow, content grows. If content grows, infrastructure must grow. If infrastructure improves, users can access, interact and generate more signals.

Explosive positive loop

U ↔ C ↔ I ↔ U

Users, content and infrastructure form a reinforcing loop. Without management variables, it tends to accelerate until friction, cost, saturation or weak management slows it down.

Forrester-inspired state update
Stock(t+1) = Stock(t) + inflow(t) - outflow(t)

In this capsule, the stocks are Users, Content and Infrastructure. The inflows are growth, production, investment and AI automation. The outflows are churn, obsolescence, technical debt, cost and exogenous friction.

References: Forrester (1961), Sterman (2000), Meadows (2008).

3 · Internet Dynamics variables

The model has three endogenous nodes and two management layers.

The canvas is intentionally simple at the center: Users, Content and Infrastructure. Complexity appears as subnodes, measures and weights attached to those three core variables.

Users · U

Potential revenue and relationship

Audience, cookies, cohorts, leads, fans, clients, promoters, IoT and AI agents. Users are the market side of communication.

Content · C

Offer, message and cost

SEO, SEM, paid media, social, email, web, product, service, brand, keywords and consumption signals. Content is value proposal and communication cost.

Infrastructure · I

Medium, channel and technical capacity

Devices, channels, workflow AI, CRM, ERP, analytics, tags, payment, ecommerce, cloud, performance and data capture. Infrastructure is also cost.

Company management

Processes, organization, systems and data/AI maturity determine whether the company can manage the digital communication system.

Exogenous ecosystem

PESTEL, legal, GDPR, competition, innovation, economic pressure and market culture accelerate or slow the system.

METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains why this connection creates value

After understanding the communication system, the feedback loop and the Internet Dynamics score, DIIIP organizes the logic: value does not appear in an isolated node. Value appears when raw data becomes a visible decision.

D
Data

Signals captured from users, content, channels, devices, journeys, searches, campaigns and business systems.

I
Information

Structured events with context: source, user type, content consumed, channel, moment, cost and expected result.

I
Intelligence

The system interprets patterns, detects gaps, reads friction and identifies where communication is losing value.

I
Insights

Actionable findings: which audience, content, channel or infrastructure variable must be improved first.

P
Personalization Actions

Operational actions that personalize the message, automate the process and move the system closer to effective communication.
